Auto-Key

With the Auto-Key checkbox armed (shown in red), every property you edit records a keyframe at the playhead — from any panel, tool drawer, arrow-key nudge, or canvas drag — creating the track on first touch. Exceptions: camera navigation (orbit / truck / wheel / HUD bars) only re-keys a camera that is already animated, so looking around never starts a camera animation; deliberate edits in the Camera panel do record. Value edits inside the timeline panel always create keyframes, armed or not — the timeline is the animation editor. “Key Selected” snapshots the full transform of the selection regardless of Auto-Key. Auto-Key always starts OFF.
